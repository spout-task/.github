Understanding how neural activity gives rise to behavior requires experimental approaches
that combine precisely quantified behavior with simultaneous recording and manipulation of
brain activity — all on a shared clock. **SPOUT** is an open-source, Teensy-driven
state-machine platform with a MATLAB interface that runs **10 unique decision-making tasks**
(with dozens of variations available through user-friendly settings) to study behavior in
head-restrained mice, and integrates seamlessly with behavioral cameras, neural recordings,
and perturbation techniques.

The platform is built on several custom hardware devices — a dual-lick detector, headplate
designs for optogenetics and two-photon calcium imaging, a three-axis motorized spout
manipulator, and an optogenetics power modulator. The firmware differentiates between one-
and two-spout tasks and is driven from a user-friendly interface; task settings can be
chosen in the interface or loaded from predefined settings files.

We validated the platform's clock speed and lick detection against an independent, external
acquisition system and found highly precise, **sub-millisecond detection of single licks**.
A pseudo-random synchronization pulse generated by SPOUT lets you correct for missing data
from acquisition glitches and clock drift — keeping behavior and neural data aligned even
over long sessions.

### Why SPOUT
- 🧠 **10 decision-making tasks**, from classical conditioning to history-dependent choice, in two state machines (one- and two-spout).
- ⚙️ **No recompiling** — every task is a shareable CSV settings file; switch tasks and tune parameters from the GUI.
- 💡 **Calibrated optogenetics** — analog optical power (mW) commanded per trial by the state machine, not a bare TTL.
- ⏱️ **Validated, sub-millisecond timing** — alignment across behavior, an independent DAQ, and two-photon imaging, with sync-pulse drift correction.
- 🔓 **Fully open and affordable** — custom hardware, firmware, GUI, and analysis code.

## The 10 tasks

**SPOUT1 — one spout:** go/no-go · stop-signal · fixed-ratio · progressive-ratio · Pavlovian conditioning

**SPOUT2 — two spouts:** uninstructed lick-left/lick-right (LL/LR) · instructed LL/LR · two-armed bandit (2ABT) · delayed-response (DR) · Pavlovian conditioning


Beyond these ten, task-specific transitions and togglable penalty states can be combined
independently within each task, so the same two state machines already implement **well over
a dozen distinct task variants** — and many more can be configured simply by changing task
variables in the MATLAB GUI, without any programming experience required.
